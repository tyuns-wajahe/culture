*Not to be confused with the city of [[Nawomapo]], sometimes referred to simply as Omapo, or the [[Omapo Federation]]*

An **Omapo** is a type of city constructed on the Iron Lakes of the Upper River, near the [[Court of the Gods]]. These cities, which all share close ties and traditions that make them distinct from the rest of the Wajahe, are typically ruled by a decentralized federation council, also called the Omapo.

There are 5 Omapos - 
- [nəw.o.mɐ.po](Nawomapo)
- o.mɐ.po ʋi.ske.ne
- o.mɐ.po uʋ.ne
- o.mɐ.po ʋæ.ʋe
- o.mɐ.po kʷwy.wɛs

Omapos are characterized by their unique architecture, preferring large stone structures resembling in spirit if not form the [Riverine](The%20Riverine%20Culture) longhouses, that house individuals by profession.
